# Race Day Detour

## Project Description

Race Day Detour pulls upcoming races into one place, shows each race's date, start time, and route on a map, and flags which major streets will be affected. You can add any race to your calendar with the route and closure notes attached, so you can plan your drive, your run, or your Saturday around it. It's built for the neighbors who are tired of finding out about a race when they hit the barricade.
